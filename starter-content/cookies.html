2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, tracking pixels and techniques that access information on a device. Their purpose and behaviour determine the applicable rules; a technology is not exempt simply because it is not called a cookie.

4. WordPress, plugins and hosting

WordPress may use essential cookies for its login and administration functions, including authentication, logged-in status and the login-page cookie test. These are managed by WordPress rather than optional tracking added by this theme. Password-protected content or additional WordPress features may introduce other cookies if the website operator uses them.

This theme provides a public enquiry form without requiring a visitor account. It does not install analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, third-party embeds or a marketing-cookie system. Security, caching, forms, analytics or other plugins may introduce further technologies; their names, providers, purposes and lifetimes need to be disclosed for the actual installed configuration.

Browser site-data settings show cookies used in your current session. Hosting services may also process technical request logs. The theme’s one-hour server-side rate-limit record is not a browser cookie; our Privacy Policy explains this limited abuse-prevention processing.

5. Consent and your choices

For storage or access technologies that require consent, we must obtain your informed, freely given choice before use. Continuing to browse, accepting general terms or submitting an enquiry is not consent to optional tracking. Relevant legal exceptions apply only where their conditions are met.

Our current application has no optional tracking categories to enable, so it does not show an “accept all” cookie banner or save a cookie-consent preference. This is not a claim that WordPress core, installed plugins or your hosting services use no cookies.

You may inspect, block or delete website data through your browser settings. Blocking authentication or security cookies may prevent WordPress administrators from logging in or affect protected features. Your browser’s autofill information is controlled through its settings.
