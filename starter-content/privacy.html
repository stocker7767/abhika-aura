3. How the enquiry form works

When you press “Send enquiry”, the form sends your name, company, email, telephone number, selected service and message to this website’s WordPress server for validation. Accepted enquiries are saved privately in the website database with a reference number before an email notification is attempted. These saved records are restricted to website administrators.

Our website tools record notification status and attempt to send the enquiry to our configured business recipient using WordPress’s mail service and any mail provider configured by the website operator. Failed notifications may be retried up to three times in total, and an administrator can request a resend. Acceptance by the mail service does not guarantee delivery to the recipient’s inbox. Your saved enquiry remains available to our administrators if a notification fails.

A mail or security plugin and hosting or email services may keep delivery logs or correspondence under their own configured arrangements. We review those arrangements and give further information where required.

For abuse prevention, the theme temporarily stores a salted hash derived from a connection’s IP address and a submission counter in WordPress transients, with a one-hour expiry. It does not store the raw IP address or enquiry message in those rate-limit records. Expired records may remain physically present until WordPress’s normal cleanup runs.

A random signed submission token helps prevent duplicate records, and a temporary confirmation reference is held on the server for up to 15 minutes. These records do not contain the raw connection IP address.

The form does not store its fields in browser cookies, local storage or session storage. Your browser may remember fields according to your settings. Email delivery is subject to the host’s mail configuration; if the form reports a failure, you can contact us directly by email or telephone.

7. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose, applicable legal requirements and the resolution of complaints or claims.

  • Website enquiry records: saved website records are scheduled for permanent deletion 365 days after submission. Cleanup and notification retries depend on WordPress scheduled tasks running; overdue tasks run when the site receives traffic or the host runs its scheduler. For an active project or a genuine need to retain information longer, relevant correspondence must be moved to the company’s separate client or legal record system before website deletion. Email copies and backups follow their own controlled retention arrangements.
  • Contract and financial records: our standard approach is up to six years after the engagement ends, or for a different period where applicable tax, accounting or legal requirements justify it. This does not mean keeping every technical file or every piece of personal data for six years.
  • Technical and security logs: the theme’s temporary rate-limit record expires after one hour; other retention depends on the provider’s operational and security arrangements. We do not specify an unverified universal retention period for hosting-platform records.

Records subject to a genuine dispute or legal hold may need to be kept longer. Unnecessary information is deleted or anonymised, subject to controlled backup cycles and lawful retention. Contact us for the retention criteria applying to your records.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights to access your personal information, correct inaccuracies, request erasure, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and receive qualifying information in a portable format.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing already lawfully carried out. You may object to direct marketing at any time. Some rights have exceptions, including where records must be kept by law or for legal claims.

We respond to rights requests without undue delay and normally within one month, subject to lawful identity checks, clarification, extensions or exemptions. We may ask for enough information to verify your identity. Requests are normally free; any lawful fee or refusal is explained.
